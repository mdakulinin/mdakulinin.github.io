Introduction

About me

I am a master's student in Data Analysis in Biology and Medicine, with an undergraduate background in biotechnology. My interests lie at the intersection of bioinformatics, population genetics, and algorithm development for evolutionary analysis.

I work with large-scale genomic datasets and am particularly interested in computationally efficient, reproducible phylogenetic methods.

Selected work

Research projects

Ongoing research

Large-scale human mtDNA phylogenetics

Reconstructing and refining phylogenetic relationships among large collections of mitochondrial genomes. Topics include sequence quality control, haplogroup references, likelihood-based placement, and scalable tree updates.

Algorithm development

Scalable phylogenetic tree construction

Exploring compressed sequence representations, cached likelihood calculations, stepwise taxon placement, and local topology refinement for large datasets.

Population genomics

Faecalibacterium comparative genomics

Comparative analysis of bacterial genomes, including species-level relationships, population structure, pangenome variation, and antimicrobial resistance annotations.

Research direction

Maternal and paternal lineage reconstruction

Studying the complementary phylogenetic information provided by mitochondrial DNA and Y-chromosomal variation in population-scale datasets.
