About me

I am currently pursuing a master's degree in Data Analysis in Biology and Medicine at HSE University, following undergraduate training in biotechnology.

My research interests center on computational approaches to evolutionary and population genetics, including large-scale phylogenetic reconstruction, genetic variation, and microbial population genomics. I enjoy connecting biological questions with algorithm design and reproducible software.
